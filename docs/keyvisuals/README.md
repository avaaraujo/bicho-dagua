# Key visuals: 6 visual directions

Generated in Krea (Nano Banana Pro), following Impeccable's method for a new visual world. The directions are drawn from the players' cultural world (adult friends, a water race, a game night), span different material families, and steer clear of the usual AI-generated looks. All art and characters are original.

| # | Direction | Material | Vehicles | Track | Three.js feasibility on mobile |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Paper boats in a rainstorm** | paper, wet asphalt | paper boats (newspaper, notebook, receipt, lottery slip) | city gutters around one block, with a storm drain as the hairpin | high: simple geometry, the scene lives on light and rain |
| 2 | **Floats at the motel water park** | glossy vinyl, chlorinated water | donut, crocodile, armchair, pizza | the lazy river, with a whirlpool on the curve | high: caustics in a shader, rounded meshes |
| 3 | **Late-90s console** | low-poly, CRT, fog | jet-skis with animal characters | a ring channel inside a volcano | very high: low-poly is cheap and nostalgic |
| 4 | **Jangadas in woodcut style** | woodcut, cordel booklets, wood | jangada rafts with patterned sails | a lagoon in Ceará marked by buoys | medium: woodcut texture needs a custom shader |
| 5 | **Plasticine capybaras** | stop-motion clay | watermelon, bathtub, lily pad, flip-flop | a mangrove river with a caiman as a bump | medium: the clay look needs good materials and normal maps |
| 6 | **Art Deco regatta** | flat lithograph, 6 inks | wooden speedboats | a bay with a lighthouse in the middle | high: flat colors, toon shading |

Scope: the race screen and the menus that surround it (title, room, lobby, results). Race = Experience; menus = Operate. Players: adult friends, desktop keyboard + phone touch.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A water race that looks like a beloved late-90s console cartridge played on a CRT, not a modern mobile racer or a franchise clone. Refuses glossy PBR, neon-on-black and flat app UI.

OWN-WORLD: Chunky flat-shaded low-poly with vertex-colour gradients, distance fog, low internal resolution upscaled with hard pixels, faint scanlines. Palette: tropical teal water (#19c3b4 to #0b7f86), magma orange (#ff6a1a), jungle green (#3fa34d), dusk violet sky (#9a7fd1 to #f3b6a0), basalt plum (#3b2440). Type: Titan One beveled cream-chrome numerals for HUD and titles, Nunito 800 for menu text.

STORY: Open a link, make or join a room with a 4-letter code, pick an animal, race 5 laps round the crater ring; corners decide the race.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Chase cam behind your jet-ski, crater walls with lava falls and waterfalls, stone whale statue in the centre. VOLTA n/5 top-left, big position bottom-right, radar minimap bottom-left, CONTRAMÃO! centre when reversed.

FORM: candidate 3 of 7 (late-90s console), user-chosen key visual; seed key 59455476.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
